# BeyondAlarm — Support

## Contact us

Email **beyondalarmai@gmail.com** with **[BA-Support]** at the start of the subject. In the app you can also
go to **Settings → Contact support**, which fills in the subject for you.

We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

## If an alarm didn't ring

Please tell us straight away, and include:

* the date and the time the alarm was set for;
* your iPhone model and iOS version *(Settings → General → About)*;
* whether Silent mode or a Focus was on, and whether the phone had restarted since the alarm was set;
* whether the alarm reads its label or plays a sound.

Please don't include the alarm's label if it's private. We don't need it to find the cause.

## Purchases

* **Restore a purchase** on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling: open BeyondAlarm → **Settings → Restore
  Purchases**.
* **Cancel a subscription:** on your iPhone, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BeyondAlarm
  Pro**. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
* **Refunds** are handled by Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>.

## Your alarms, timers and recordings

They are stored only on your iPhone. They don't sync to your other devices. When you move to a new
iPhone, restoring it from an iCloud or computer backup brings them across.

## Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
